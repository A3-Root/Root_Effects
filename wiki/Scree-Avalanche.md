# Scree Avalanche

- Dust and scree cascade down the slope (downhill automatically, or a set heading).
- **Boulders** (0-80): big boulders released from the head of the slide on top of the dust and scree. They fall, bounce off the terrain and roll downhill until they settle. Their paths are simulated from a shared seed on every machine, so every player sees the same boulders in the same place and the damage matches what they see.
- **Lethal**: a moving boulder that runs into someone deals damage scaled by its speed and size and knocks them along. Vehicles take hitpoint and hull damage, their crews are hurt, and they get shoved.
- Optional extra props (vehicle class names) can be rolled down with it.
