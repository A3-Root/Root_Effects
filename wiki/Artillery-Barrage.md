# Artillery Barrage

Modes: lethal (real shells), non-lethal (visual impacts) and sound only.

Visual impacts:
- a flak-style flash with a large lens flare that pops and fades;
- a fireball, sparks and embers, and clods of earth on ballistic arcs;
- a dust ring blasted along the ground;
- a thick dark smoke column and smouldering crater smoke.

Each impact plays its double report from its own sound source, so the next shell never cuts the previous one off. Everything scales with the client particle budget.
